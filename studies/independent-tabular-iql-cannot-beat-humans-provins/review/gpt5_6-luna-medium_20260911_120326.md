## Understanding of the hypothesis

- **Hypothesis in my own words:** In this one Provins scenario, with 40% selfish CAVs and the specified tabular IQL setup, independently learning CAVs will not reliably obtain a meaningfully lower test travel time than the learned human-routing baseline.
- **Tested setting:** Provins, fixed 523-agent demand, 209 CAVs, `selfish_40`, four generated routes, `trip_info_eta`, bin width 20, 300 human-learning days, 800 CAV-learning days, 100 test days, and paired seeds 40–43 for the environment and tabular learners.
- **Main metric and meaningful-effect threshold:** `Effect_of_change = t_HDV_pre / t_CAV`; a ratio of at least 1.02 is meaningful.
- **Rules for support, falsification, and an inconclusive result:** Support requires median ratio at most 1.00 and at most one of four runs reaching 1.02. Falsification requires median at least 1.02 and at least three runs reaching 1.02. Otherwise the result is inconclusive, including incomparable runs.
- **I understand the hypothesis and how it is tested:** yes.

## Review

### Experiment design

The design is adequate for the deliberately narrow question. The four runs hold the algorithm, demand, task, observation, route count, training length, and test length fixed, and compare CAVs with the human population from the same run. Four seed pairs are a lightweight but coherent replication set for the stated decision rule. The retrospective nature is a real limitation, but it is disclosed rather than presented as preregistration. Shared numeric environment/model seeds reduce independence between those sources of randomness, and the selected hyperparameters came from earlier tuning; neither is hidden.

The main comparison is not a matched-agent counterfactual: RouteRL's `t_HDV_pre` is computed over the 314 agents that remain human after mutation, whereas `t_CAV` is computed over the 209 converted agents. Because mutation is random and agents have fixed trips, this can introduce trip-composition differences. It is a limitation of the effect measure, not an unacknowledged change to the stated rule.

### Runs and evidence

All four named run directories are present and internally consistent. Each has a saved configuration, 240 saved episode records (every 5 days through day 1200), raw SUMO and RouteRL outputs, combined data, benchmark metrics, plots, and the expected 523/209 population split. The configurations match the proposal exactly. No failed or excluded run is identified in the supplied family, and the report gives per-seed values rather than only an aggregate.

The benchmark rows reproduce the report: `Effect_of_change` is 1.0407, 1.1244, 1.0966, and 1.0697 for seeds 40–43, with median 1.0830. The study does not record the Python or SUMO versions or a standalone analysis script, so reproducibility outside this repository is incomplete, but the underlying data needed to audit the reported metric are present.

### Analysis and conclusion

The calculations are correct. Direct recomputation of `t_HDV_pre / t_CAV` from the saved benchmark rows matches every stored ratio, and all four exceed 1.02. The median exceeds 1.02, so the proposal's falsification rule is met. The report accurately limits the conclusion to the specified Provins configuration and does not claim that independent learning generally beats humans.

As a robustness check on the population-composition concern, recomputing the pre-mutation baseline for the same 209 agents later labeled CAV gives ratios of approximately 1.063, 1.060, 1.059, and 1.073; this is exploratory review analysis, not part of the study's prespecified rule, but it does not reverse the reported direction. The report is also appropriately candid that the decision rules and configuration were selected retrospectively.

### Strongest challenge

The strongest challenge is selection and baseline validity: the study chose a favorable pre-existing tuned configuration after results existed, and its main metric compares converted agents with a different set of remaining humans rather than their own pre-mutation performance. Thus the result should not be read as an unbiased estimate of tabular IQL's general advantage. Within the exact scoped setting and stated metric, however, the raw artifacts show a consistent effect in all four runs, and the same-agent pre-period check does not undermine the direction.

### Serious problems

None. The retrospective design, shared seed values, missing software-version metadata, and non-counterfactual baseline limit generality and reproducibility, but they are disclosed or auditable and do not make the scoped conclusion untrustworthy.
